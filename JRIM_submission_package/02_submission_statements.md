## 6. Cross-sectional design and causal restraint — insert/retain in Methodology and Limitations

> Because the study uses a cross-sectional, self-reported survey design, the
> findings should be interpreted as evidence of theoretically specified
> associations rather than definitive causal effects. Terms such as "pathway"
> and "mechanism" are used in a model-based explanatory sense and should not
> be read as experimental proof.

*Author action: after inserting this, audit the full text (especially the
abstract, results and discussion) and soften any remaining causal verbs

hypothesis statements.*

## 7. H5 pathway comparison — use in the Results section

> Using the reported pathway-level variance partition, the moral-relational
> pathway accounted for an estimated 21.4 percentage points of explained
> variance in customer satisfaction, compared with approximately 13.1
> percentage points for the instrumental pathway. H5 is therefore supported as
> a model-based explanatory comparison.

*Author actions before submission:*
1. Verify 21.4 and 13.1 against the final PLS output (re-run if the model
